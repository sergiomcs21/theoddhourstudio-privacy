Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile apps and games published by The Odd Hour Studio ("we", "us"). It explains what data our apps collect and how it is used.

Our apps do not require an account, do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information, and do not let you create a profile.

Data collected automatically

Our apps may use the following third-party services, which collect limited technical data:

  • Google AdMob — shows banner, interstitial, and/or rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to infer an approximate region), and interaction with ads, to select and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — collects anonymous, aggregated usage events (such as which game mode was played, final score, or feature usage) to help us understand how our apps are used. This data is not tied to your name or any account.

Data stored on your device

Our apps typically store your scores and settings locally on your device, and this data is never sent to us. Uninstalling an app removes this data.

Your choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you're located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, our apps show a consent form on first launch letting you choose whether to allow personalized ads and analytics. You can review or change this choice at any time from the app's Settings → Privacy Options menu, where available.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Scope

This policy covers all current and future apps published under The Odd Hour Studio name. If a specific app collects data beyond what is described here (for example, accounts or in-app purchases), that app's store listing will note the difference and this page will be updated accordingly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at theoddhourstudio@gmail.com.
